Da FIFA a EA SPORTS FC

Explain what the EA transition illustrates about licensing and control. Choose one right the firm could retain separately and one decision it would face when entering a foreign market.

Ripassa le licenze · Fonte del caso EA

Controlla il ragionamento
  • Define licensing and identify the specific asset or right.
  • Separate the FIFA name from individual partner agreements. The press release documents a distinction, not every contract term.
  • Compare one plausible entry option with a criterion such as control, capital or exposure.

La filiera di una fotocamera

An illustrative camera brand designs in one country and contracts a supplier elsewhere. A tariff is introduced on an imported component. Trace the effect from supplier to retailer and compare two responses.

Ripassa la catena del valore

Controlla il ragionamento
  • Map design, sourcing, assembly and distribution before discussing the tariff.
  • Identify who imports the component and distinguish statutory payment from economic incidence.
  • Discuss how contractual power and substitutability affect pass-through, then compare two responses with explicit assumptions.

Streetwear in un mercato nuovo

An illustrative independent fashion label wants to enter a foreign market. Compare exporting, licensing and a joint venture. Recommend a route and state what evidence would make you change your mind.

Ripassa le modalità di entrata

Controlla il ragionamento
  • Compare the alternatives using the same dimensions: cost, control, market knowledge and risk.
  • Explain the specific role of a potential local partner and distinguish rights from ownership.
  • Make a conditional recommendation and identify observable evidence that could reverse it.
